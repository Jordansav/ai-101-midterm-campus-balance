# ai-101-midterm-campus-balance
I plan to create a website called Campus Balance, this will be a time management platform designed to help college students organize their academics and personal other responsibilities. The goal is to reduce stress by providing one place where students can view and manage everything they need to do.


Audience: College students and athletes who want to organize their academics, time, and extra curricular activities all in one app easily accessible by phone and computer


Purpose:
To create an all in one app easily accessible by phone and computer that will be a time management platform designed to help college students organize their academics and personal other responsibilities. The website would be especially useful for student-athletes who often have demanding practice and travel schedules but it would also work well for any college student trying to balance classes, homework, work shifts, and extracurricular activities.


First Features:
First, users could create a personalized schedule by entering their class times, work shifts, practices, and other commitments into a calendar. Second, students could create and organize assignments with due dates, priorities, and reminders to help them stay on track. Third, the website would provide a dashboard that highlights upcoming deadlines and available study time, making it easier to plan each week effectively.


How to Run it:
Users can open featured calendar and enter upcoming events or due dates for assignments/ test/ Practice/ Games. Dashboard will also feature upcoming deadlines and available study time making it easier to plan each week effectively.
